> The word tie can be misleadingly referring to the score

**Redrafted:** 2026-08-15 (daily-reel-render)

Removed "tie" everywhere and used the rulebook's own word, "simultaneously",
in its place. Ultimate plays to a points cap, so "tie" reads as a scoreline
before it reads as two players catching at once, and 12.3 never uses the word.

- Title: "Tie goes to the offence" → "Caught at the same time? Offence keeps
  it." The cover now states the situation instead of borrowing the idiom.
- Scene 2 headline "Both of you caught it. It's theirs." → "Both of you have
  it. It's theirs."; its body no longer calls 12.3 a "tie-breaker".
- Scene 5 headline "First hands wins. A tie is rarer than it feels." → "First
  hands wins. Truly simultaneous is rare."; body now says the two catches
  "were never simultaneous" rather than "was never a tie".
- Both captions reworked the same way — "the genuine dead heat" became "the
  genuine same-instant catch", and the TikTok opener now reads "you both caught
  it at the same instant".
- Rule 12.3 is untouched and still the only citation; lesson number, difficulty
  and hashtags unchanged.
