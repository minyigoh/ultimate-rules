### Why the title is "the last five lessons"

It is the plainest true description of the deck, and on this particular deck the
plainest true description is also the most interesting thing about it: the
seventy-five-lesson course finishes here.

**The title was checked against the last slide, not just the theme of the first
five**, which is the carousel-post-6 v1 defect. That check is trivially passed
here and worth stating anyway: "the last five lessons" is a count, not a theme,
so there is no slide it can exclude. A thematic title would have struggled —
this block is two lessons about the things around the game (divisions, kit) and
three about how you treat the people in it, and any phrase covering all five
honestly would have been looser than the count.
