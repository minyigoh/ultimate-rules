## Round 1 — 2026-09-01 (Content Desk, via the content track)

**Status set:** `rerender` on the content track, tagged "Wrong take" — treated
as a script round, because it asks for different words rather than a different
render. Same routing as reel-11 round 1.

> Out loud may not be appropriate. We use hand signals in ultimate frisbee.
> Practically, we shout and use hand signals.

**Redrafted:** 2026-09-01 (daily-reel-render)

The note is correct and the rulebook is on its side. **15.7** says players stop
play by "visibly or audibly communicating the stoppage" — the disjunction is
the rule's own — and **15.13** says players "are encouraged to use the WFDF
Hand Signals to communicate all calls". v1 described only the audible half, and
described it as the whole thing.

What changed:

- **Scene 8, the field tip.** "Call it at the moment of contact, out loud." →
  "Call it at the moment of contact — shout and signal." The body no longer
  stops at *don't wait*; it now says what the call actually consists of, and
  why the signal is the half that carries — downfield players cannot hear a
  call across a windy sideline, but they can see it, and 15.7's echo depends on
  their seeing it.
- **Scene 6's kicker.** `SAY IT IMMEDIATELY` → `CALL IT IMMEDIATELY`. "Say"
  smuggles the same voice-only assumption into the one scene that is *about*
  timeliness. "Call" is the rulebook's verb and is modality-neutral. 573px →
  603px of the 900px column at the standard 34px; nothing near `fit_kicker()`.
- **Both captions.** The Instagram caption's "All of which depends on saying it
  in time" became "making the call in time", and gained a short paragraph on
  saying-and-signalling. The TikTok caption gained the equivalent line. The
  block-quoted rule text in both is untouched.
- **The example beat** now ends on the word and the signal together.

What deliberately did **not** change:

- **No new citation.** 15.7 and 15.13 are the obvious rules to reach for and
  both are off-limits here: they belong to **lesson 73, "Use your hands"**,
  which cites exactly those two plus 1.3.7. Carding them on lesson 28 would
  pre-empt a lesson that is already written and would quietly widen what this
  reel claims to cover. The field tip carries no citation footer, so the point
  is made in plain English and stays practical, which is what a field tip is
  for.
- **Rule numbers, lesson number, difficulty, hashtags, attribution** — all
  unchanged. 17.2.1, 17.2.1.1, 17.2.2 and 15.8 are still the four, still quoted
  verbatim from `rules.json`.
- **Every rules card.** Nothing on a citation card moved; this was only ever a
  problem in the copy the account writes itself.

Previous copy archived as `script-and-caption.v1.md`.

The rendered v1 was built from the superseded copy and is **not** being
regenerated in this pass — words clear the first gate before anything is
re-rendered. The content track stays where the desk left it and the calendar
row stays at "Content rejected — regenerate"; the next run rebuilds once this
redraft is approved.
