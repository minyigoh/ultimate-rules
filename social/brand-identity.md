Pulled from minyigoh.github.io/ultimate-rules so the social presence reads as the same brand, not a spin-off. "Ultimate Frisbee Academy" was taken, so the name landed on **Learn Ultimate Frisbee** — keeps the "Learn" hook from the original site name, still broad enough to cover future pillars beyond rules (strategy, mindset, workouts).

## Visual style

Lifted directly from the site's CSS/theme so posts feel like an extension of it, not a mismatched sibling account.

| Element | Value | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Background (dark) | `#0F1712` | Site's dark-mode theme colour — near-black deep green. Use as the base for video backgrounds/end cards. |
| Background (light) | `#F1F3EE` | Off-white sage — site's light mode. Good for text-on-light caption cards. |
| Accent | `#E24A12` | Burnt orange — used for chapter numbers, rule citations, highlights. This is *the* brand colour; use it for CTAs, underlines, and the "today's rule" badge look. |

Suggested video template: dark `#0F1712` background, white body text, orange `#E24A12` for the hook line and rule-number citations, one clean sans-serif throughout.

## Voice

**Calm-coach with dry wit** — matches the site's tone exactly: plain-English, respects the viewer's intelligence, short declarative sentences, the occasional deadpan aside. Not hype-hypebeast, not meme-heavy, no forced exclamation points.

Reference lines straight from the site that capture it:
- *"Two teams of seven. You score by catching a pass inside the end zone you are attacking — that's the entire objective."*
- *"There is no stoppage, no reset, no whistle. Possession just flips."*
- *"Wait — what just happened?"* (their own FAQ section header — good model for hook lines)

Do:
- Explain the rule, then the *why*, then a concrete example.
- Use rule numbers as receipts ("this is 9.3, not a house rule").
- Let a dry one-liner land without over-explaining it.

Don't:
- Oversell ("you WON'T BELIEVE this rule").
- Talk down to beginners or over-explain jargon after defining it once.
- Promise virality or growth numbers in captions — see Reality Check below.

## Attribution (non-negotiable, every post)

The rule text and annotations are WFDF's, not ours. Every caption footer or the bio link should carry something like:

> Rule source: WFDF Rules of Ultimate 2025–2028, used under CC BY 4.0.

Update the year range whenever WFDF publishes a new rules cycle — don't let this go stale.

## Reality check

Consistency and a clearly useful angle raise the odds of traction. "Viral" is a possible outcome of doing this well for months, never a metric to promise in a caption, a bio, or to yourself. No growth projections get baked into calendar or pillar docs — only actual reported numbers once you have them.

## Cadence

Post daily; write in weekly batches of 7 (script + both captions + hashtags), held in "Pending review" in the content calendar until you approve/edit each one. Nothing goes out un-reviewed.
